About the Project

Tahseel App is the ideal solution for middle and high school students, providing a comprehensive educational experience that combines smart learning, entertainment, and rewards to help students achieve the best academic results in a fun and effective way.

The app aims to facilitate lesson follow-up, organize review schedules, and practice interactive learning through various challenges and tests, in addition to opportunities to obtain real cash prizes upon excellence and continuous participation.

Core Advantages

Offline Access

Students can browse study materials, lessons, and books without needing an internet connection, allowing study anytime and anywhere.

Cash Prizes

The app offers incentive cash prizes for students who achieve outstanding performance in tests, motivating them to continue learning.

Educational Challenges

Includes weekly and monthly competitions and challenges that help students test their skills with a touch of fun and competition.

Digital Library

Provides all approved textbooks for middle and high school, with the ability to search, read, and save important notes easily.

Diverse Questions

Contains multiple types of questions (MCQ, True/False) with model answers and simplified explanations to ensure full understanding.
